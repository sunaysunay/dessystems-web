How it works

A repeatable process, not a photo session.

  1. 1

    Check in

    Vehicle is registered by VIN or plate and linked to your stock record.

  2. 2

    Capture

    Exterior, 360°, interior and inspection shots follow a fixed shot list.

  3. 3

    Process

    AI handles background, cropping and quality checks against your brand rules.

  4. 4

    Publish

    Image sets sync to your website, DMS and marketplace listings.
